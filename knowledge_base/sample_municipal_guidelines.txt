Municipal Waste Segregation Guidelines (Sample)

1. Wet waste includes food scraps, fruit peels, and garden trimmings. Use the green bin or compost.
2. Dry waste includes clean paper, cardboard, and approved plastics. Keep materials dry.
3. Glass bottles should be rinsed and placed in glass recycling where available.
4. E-waste such as mobile phones and computers must go to authorized collection centers.
5. Batteries are hazardous waste. Never dispose in household trash; use battery drop-off points.
6. Plastic bottles labeled PET are commonly recyclable after rinsing.
7. Local rules may differ; contact your municipal corporation for collection schedules.
